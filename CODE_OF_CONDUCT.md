# Code of Conduct

Reliable AI Lab welcomes technical discussion and contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their technical merits.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and assume good faith.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and research integrity.
- Make room for newcomers to ask questions and learn.
- Disagree without personal attacks, harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized conduct, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information is not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project constructive and safe. Security vulnerabilities should follow SECURITY.md rather than being posted publicly.
