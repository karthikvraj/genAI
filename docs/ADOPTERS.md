# Adopters and research use

This page is intentionally evidence-based. Organizations, teams and researchers are listed only after they independently confirm meaningful use of Reliable AI Lab.

## Confirmed adopters

_No confirmed external adopters are listed yet._

## How to be listed

Open an issue or pull request describing:

- the organization, team or research group name you are permitted to publish;
- which Reliable AI Lab capability you use;
- a public reference when available (repository, paper, article, talk or project page); and
- whether the project may quote a short factual description of the use.

Private usage does not need to be disclosed. We will not infer or claim company adoption from stars, forks, downloads, employment, or private conversations.
